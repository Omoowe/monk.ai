How much page below the hero?

Validation pages work best when focused. Pick the depth.

A

Minimal — 3 sections

Hero → 3 punchy reasons → Email capture

Ships in hours. Ruthlessly focused. Forces the copy to carry the weight. Best for pure email validation — do people care enough to give their email?

B

Medium — 5 sections

Hero → Problem statement → 3 key features → Personality modes preview → Waitlist CTA

1–2 days to build well. Shows enough product to validate the concept, not just the headline. Personality modes section is the virality hook.

C

Full — 8+ sections

Hero → Problem → How it works → All 6 personalities → Features → Monk Mode spotlight → Social proof → Pricing preview → Waitlist

3–5 days. Closer to a real product site. High conversion potential but overkill pre-validation — you don't have social proof yet.

Recommendation: B (Medium) — hero alone won't explain the personality modes differentiation, which is the viral hook. But don't build pricing or social proof sections yet — you have neither.
