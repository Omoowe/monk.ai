Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Effective immediately

Monk.ai ("Monk", "we", "us") is an AI-powered accountability coaching application. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, how it is used, and your rights over it. We keep this plain — no jargon designed to confuse.

01

What We Collect

Account Data

  • Email address (used for authentication)
  • Display name (provided by you during onboarding)
  • Password (stored as a secure hash — we never see it in plaintext)

Coaching & Usage Data

  • Habits: names, emoji, completion history, streak counts
  • Goals: name, category, progress, target, deadline
  • Check-ins: morning missions, evening debriefs, energy levels, self-reported distractions
  • Chat messages sent to and received from your AI coach
  • Coach memory: a short text summary your coach builds over time from your conversations
  • Identity statement: the personal mantra you set during onboarding
  • Chosen coach personality, Monk Mode status, dopamine score, streak

Technical Data

  • Device type and operating system (mobile app only)
  • App version
  • Anonymised crash and error logs
  • Daily message count (used to enforce free-tier limits)
02

How We Use It

  • To deliver coaching — your habits, goals, check-ins, and memory are sent to our AI system to generate personalised, contextual coaching responses.
  • To calculate your stats — streak, dopamine score, and completion rates are computed from your habit and check-in data.
  • To enforce subscription limits — free-tier message limits are tracked server-side.
  • To maintain your account — authentication, session management, and account deletion.
  • To improve Monk — aggregated, anonymised usage patterns (never individual content) may inform product decisions.

We do not sell your data. We do not run ads. We do not use your coaching conversations for marketing.

03

AI Processing

Your coaching conversations are processed by Anthropic (Claude AI) via our server-side Supabase Edge Functions. This means:

  • Your messages and context are sent to Anthropic's API to generate a response.
  • Anthropic processes this data under their own Privacy Policy.
  • Your Anthropic API key is never stored in or exposed to the client app — all AI calls go through our secure backend.
  • We only send the minimum context needed: your name, active habits/goals, today's mission, and coach memory.

Anthropic's enterprise API does not use customer inputs to train models by default. See their privacy documentation for current commitments.

05

Data Retention

  • Account and coaching data is retained as long as your account is active.
  • If you delete your account, all associated data — habits, goals, check-ins, chat history, coach memory — is permanently deleted from our database within 30 days.
  • Anonymised aggregate statistics may be retained indefinitely.
  • Backups are purged on a rolling 30-day cycle.
06

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction — update inaccurate data directly in the app or by contacting us.
  • Deletion — delete your account and all associated data via Settings → Delete Account inside the app.
  • Portability — request an export of your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Objection — object to certain processing activities.

To exercise any right not available in-app, email us at privacy@monk.ai. We respond within 30 days.

07

Security

  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS.
  • Passwords are hashed — never stored in plaintext.
  • API keys (Anthropic, RevenueCat) are stored exclusively in server-side environment variables.
  • Row-level security (RLS) policies on Supabase ensure users can only access their own data.
  • Webhook signatures are verified using constant-time comparison to prevent timing attacks.
08

Children

Monk.ai is not directed at children under 13 (or 16 in the EU/EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us at privacy@monk.ai and we will delete the account immediately.

09

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the product evolves. Material changes will be communicated via in-app notification or email at least 14 days before they take effect. Continued use of Monk.ai after the effective date constitutes acceptance.
